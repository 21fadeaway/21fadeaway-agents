# Local business pack

For a cafe, studio, salon or shop with a physical location.

| Agent | What it does for you |
| --- | --- |
| [21 Social Media Strategist](../agents/marketing/21-social-media-strategist.md) | Plans what to post and when. |
| [21 SEO Specialist](../agents/marketing/21-seo-specialist.md) | Helps people nearby find you. |
| [21 Customer Service](../agents/specialized/21-customer-service.md) | Replies to reviews and messages. |
| [21 Bookkeeper & Controller](../agents/finance/21-bookkeeper-controller.md) | Keeps the monthly numbers clear. |

## Install

```bash
./install.sh local-business
```

Then ask by name, for example: "Use 21 Social Media Strategist to review our homepage."
